Machine Learning Platform Engineer — Training Infrastructure

around them, and the tooling the team depends on day to day.

What we look for
- Hands-on experience with multi-GPU training (FSDP, DeepSpeed or equivalent) and the
  failure modes that come with it.
- Comfort profiling collective communication and GPU utilisation.
- A track record of shipping infrastructure other engineers rely on.
- Writing that teaches: design docs, postmortems, or public technical writing.
- Strong systems fundamentals; most of our team came from backend or infra rather than
  research.
